04 / MISSION BRIEFING

KNOW THE SYSTEM.

Does a higher market cap automatically pay more fees?

No. Creator fees are generated by eligible trading volume, using the applicable fee rates. Market cap is not revenue.

Are burns automatic today?

No. This site is a functional design preview. A deployed fee collector, signer, execution service, and on-chain transaction monitor are required before automatic burns can begin.

Can MOON? supply be permanently reduced?

Yes. Tokens controlled by an authorized wallet can be destroyed using Solana's appropriate token-program burn instruction, reducing the token supply. It does not guarantee price appreciation.

What happens before MARS? launches?

The proposed MARS? allocation remains segregated in treasury accounting until the token and its market exist. No MARS? burn should be reported before a verifiable transaction occurs.

How do holder rewards work?

The 20% allocation is a proposal, not an active yield program. Exact eligibility, custody, disclosures, local legal requirements and Pump.fun platform terms must be reviewed before enabling distributions.

Is this associated with SpaceX or Tesla?

No. The visual language is inspired by modern aerospace and electric vehicle design. MOON? is independent and is not endorsed by or affiliated with SpaceX, Tesla, Elon Musk, or NASA.
